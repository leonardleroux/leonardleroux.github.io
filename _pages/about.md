I am an Assistant Professor in the [Applied Economics Department at Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona](https://www.uab.cat/en/applied-economics). Previously I worked at the [World Bank Group](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/home) in the Development Economics Private Markets Department. I obtained my PhD in Economics from [Sciences Po Paris](https://www.sciencespo.fr/department-economics/) in June 2025.

My research interests are in development, urban and environmental economics. At a broad level, I am interested in the interactions of rapid urbanization with governance and state capacity in lower and middle income countries.

You can find my CV [(here)](https://leonardleroux.github.io/files/Leonard%20le%20Roux.pdf). Email: leonard.leroux[at]uab.cat
